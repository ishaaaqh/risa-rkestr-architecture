# Data Retention

## Work Items

Soft delete first.

Default restoration window:
- 1 day

Permanent deletion:
- asynchronous

## Audit

Audit retention is independent from Work Item retention.

Audit records must remain available according to configured enterprise/compliance policy even after business data is permanently removed, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

## Attachments

Attachment lifecycle follows the owning business resource and storage lifecycle policies.
